# INSTRUCTIONS FOR CHAITRA M G - ADDING YOUR PORTRAIT PHOTO

Your personal portfolio is currently using the stylized vector placeholder:
`assets/images/chaitra-portrait-placeholder.svg`

To add your own real photograph:
1. Copy your portrait photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP format) into this folder (`assets/images/`).
2. Name it `chaitra-portrait.jpg` (or `.png`).
3. Open `index.html` in your editor.
4. Locate the hero image tag (around line 75):
   `<img src="assets/images/chaitra-portrait-placeholder.svg" alt="Chaitra M G" ...>`
5. Update `src` to your new photo:
   `<img src="assets/images/chaitra-portrait.jpg" alt="Chaitra M G" ...>`

Tip: A portrait with a clean, dark, or transparent background works best to maximize the cinematic neon glow effects!
